隐私政策

最后更新：2026-10-06

二维码&条形码（以下简称“本应用”）非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

我们收集的数据

无。本应用不收集、不上传到任何服务器、不分享任何个人信息。本应用没有账号体系、没有后端服务器，也没有任何第三方统计、广告或追踪 SDK。

保存在您设备上的数据

扫码与生成的历史记录、以及应用偏好（语言、主题、扫码声音/震动）仅保存在您的设备本地，不会离开您的设备，您可随时在应用内删除。

相机

本应用仅在您扫描二维码/条形码时使用相机，且全部在设备本地实时处理。相机画面不会被录制、保存或上传。

相册

在您授权后，本应用可读取您选择的相册图片以识别其中的码，并可将生成的码图片保存到相册。这些操作均在本地进行，本应用不会上传您的照片。

网络

唯一的网络行为是可选且由您主动触发的：当您使用“扫描网络图片”并输入网址时，本应用会下载该图片以识别其中的码。除此之外本应用不发起任何网络请求。

跳转其他应用

当您对扫码结果进行操作（打开链接、拨打电话、发送邮件/短信、查看地图、添加联系人、复制 WiFi 密码）时，本应用会将请求交给对应的系统应用处理。

儿童

本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

我们可能更新本政策，“最后更新”日期将反映相应变更。

联系方式

有关本政策的问题：mxnzp_tools@163.com

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

QR & Barcode (“the App”) is designed to protect your privacy. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. There is no account system, no backend server, and no third‑party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your scan/generation history and app preferences (language, theme, scan sound/vibration) are stored locally on your device only. They never leave your device and can be deleted anytime within the App.

Camera

The App uses the camera only to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time, on your device. Camera images are processed locally and are not recorded, stored, or transmitted.

Photos

With your permission, the App can read an image you choose to detect a code in it, and can save a generated code image to your library. These actions happen locally; the App does not upload your photos.

Network

The only network activity is optional and user‑initiated: when you use “Scan from image URL” and enter a web address, the App downloads that image to detect a code in it. The App makes no other network requests.

Opening other apps

When you act on a scan result (open a link, call, send an email/SMS, view a map, add a contact, copy a Wi‑Fi password), the App hands the request to the corresponding system app.

Children

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will reflect any changes.
